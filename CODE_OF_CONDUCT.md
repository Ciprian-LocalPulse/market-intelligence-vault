# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Challenge methods and evidence, not personal characteristics.
Use clear, constructive language; acknowledge limitations and credit others' work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and disclosure of confidential research are unacceptable.

Apply these expectations in issues, reviews, discussions and other project spaces. Maintainer
Ciprian Ștefan Pleșca may request corrections, moderate content or restrict participation according
to severity and context. Report concerns through a private GitHub contact channel when available;
do not publish identifying details or sensitive allegations in public issues. No private email
address or guaranteed response time is represented by this policy.
