## Upside

Resolve demand and delivery uncertainty. Success targets are three priced pilot commitments and
at least 30% median task-time reduction, both hypotheses until tested. No revenue forecast.

## Downside

Research spend could produce no viable offer. Connector capacity risk is 75 High; procurement
risk is 66 High. The second connector and access review are prerequisites to a pilot stage.

## Confidence

59/100 Low within this synthetic case. Unproven payment and sampling limitations constrain
the recommendation. Synthetic evidence supports no real-world conclusion.

## Alternatives

WAIT conserves cash until feasibility improves but produces less new evidence. Full ENTER is
premature because paid demand and sustainable economics are unobserved.

## Recommended Action

Release only discovery resources if approved. Stop or WAIT if the second connector exceeds 15
engineer days, access remains unresolved, or no qualified workflow baseline can be measured.

the security questionnaire. Strategy lead: review the gate and explicitly approve, narrow or stop
