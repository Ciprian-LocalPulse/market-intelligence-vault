# Roadmap

All items below are **PLANNED — NOT CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED** unless explicitly identified as existing.
They are proposals, not committed delivery dates or operational services. Priorities depend on research,
resources, rights and threat review.

## 1.1.x — Dashboard refinement

Timed executive/analyst usability studies; broader screen-reader and browser testing; print refinements;
clearer dense-table navigation; presentation consistency. The 14-section dashboard, claim drilldown,
ordinal risk matrix and single-factor ±1 opportunity sensitivity already exist in 1.1.0.

## 1.2.0 — Research Lifecycle Release

Evidence supersession; field-level revision history; observation-to-many-claim relationships;
decision-impact propagation; normalized source editing; reviewed joint-factor sensitivity and scenario
loss ranges with explicit assumptions. Current maps distinguish support, contradiction and context,
but the current evidence record binds one claim; do not mistake a map table for implemented many-claim
lifecycle support. Loss ranges would remain scenario estimates unless independently calibrated.

## 2.0.0 — Operated Intelligence Platform

Rights-aware ingestion; authenticated access; role-based approval; monitored intelligence updates;
evidence lifecycle automation; source licensing controls; longitudinal outcome collection and research
into calibrated decision models. Authentication of a user would not automatically authenticate a source.
Model calibration would require data, a stated evaluation protocol and measured outcomes before claims.

for the conditions under which a future capability may be labeled released.
