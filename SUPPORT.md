# Support and project discussions

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ciprian-LocalPulse/market-intelligence-vault/issues) for
reproducible defects, feature proposals and research/methodology questions. Use the matching form,
include a version and environment, and provide a minimal fictional example. For general questions,
open a clearly labeled question issue; Discussions may be used if the owner enables that feature.

Vulnerabilities belong in the private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post
client records, source archives, personal data or credentials. Reports and community contributions
are reviewed by the maintainer; no paid support arrangement or response-time promise is implied.
