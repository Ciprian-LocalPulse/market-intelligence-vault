# Audit report — Market Intelligence Vault 1.0.0

Audit date: 2026-10-06. Baseline: Foundation Release 1.0.0. Remediation: Quality Hardening Release 1.0.1.

## 1. Executive Verdict

**1.0.0 should not be delivered as a premium decision-ready intelligence system without hardening.**
It is a substantial professional consulting framework with executable scores and a complete synthetic
case. It is not a verified market dataset or an operated intelligence product. Baseline tests and
structural validation pass, yet deliberate probes reproduce private-file export, live approvals backed
by speculation and contract traversal. These are material trust failures, not cosmetic preferences.

This review evaluates the actual local code and records in a separate audit phase. It was performed
by the same assistant that authored the baseline; it is **not organizationally independent or externally
signed assurance**. Reviewer-role framing does not change that limitation. No real-market source verification,
empirical calibration, external reader study or legal/privacy certification was performed.

**Verified post-fix assessment: 1.0.1 is suitable for controlled professional delivery as a framework with
clearly fictional demonstrations.** It does not approve
a live market conclusion or real capital decision. A live engagement needs its own research and bound
manual reviews. Final issue statuses and checks are recorded in RELEASE_VERIFICATION_1.0.1.md.

## 3. What Is Strong

- The twelve-domain architecture covers sources, customer/competitor/market analysis, uncertainty and decisions.
- Stable IDs, critical-support maps, exact locators and explicit synthetic markers are useful foundations.
- Most arithmetic is transparent and reproducible. Pressure and risk confidence remain distinct from exposure.
- The fictional case keeps unknown paid demand visible and proposes a reversible TEST rather than fabricating revenue.
- Standard-library tooling is inspectable and does not require network access or a dependency install.

These strengths do not compensate for the reproduced release-stopping paths.

## 4. What Is Weak

The implementation originally enforces less than its prose claims. A claim label, approval status,
origin group or source URL can be syntactically valid without proving truth or authority. The initial
suite tests formula outputs and common structural failures but misses confidentiality and live gating.
Scoring inputs often have only an object-level evidence list rather than a basis for each judgment.
The repository emphasizes completeness of documents more than ease of repeated source revisions.
The original 93/100 self-review therefore cannot be interpreted as professional release assurance.

## 9. Formula Review

| Model | Baseline assessment | 1.0.1 action / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confidence | Weights sum 100; range/caps mostly work, but A ignores the directness input and strong current evidence can support a speculative FACT | Directness/source/scope caps, typed claim caps, eligible corroboration and contradiction semantics; heuristics remain uncalibrated |

All inputs are checked for missing, finite, negative and out-of-range values. Zero is a value, not an
unknown. Integer categories reject fractional values. Unrounded values define categories; stored
values use two decimals. A rounded display near a threshold can resemble the next category, so the
stored category is authoritative and must be displayed with the value. Sensitivity should perturb
uncertain ordinal factors by at least ±1 rather than treating hundredths as measured differences.

Baseline opportunity sample 61.66/52.12/40.06 becomes 52.48/47.40/33.53 under the explicitly revised
method. Risk exposure stays 75/66/72. Attractiveness 53.20 and pressure 66.00/60.80/55.60 are retained.
Cross-version rank comparison requires recomputation. Correlation among demand/pain/monetization
and between likelihood/velocity/detection remains a methodological limit; no independence is claimed.

## 10. Evidence Review

A–G is usable only when status, observation strength and claim confidence are separated. Grade G
and verified irrelevant evidence can both have score 0 for different reasons. Ordered grade rules
resolve numeric overlap, but readers must see the status. Grade A originally accepted directness 10;
the new rule requires 20 and correct scope. Recency cannot overcome zero source reliability/relevance.

Contradictions no longer automatically make an observed source statement weak; they cap the disputed
claim. HYPOTHESIS is capped at 59 even when its surrounding observations are strong. ESTIMATE is capped
at 74 and needs assumptions. FACT requires verified direct support. The estimator and reviewer still
have to decide whether the source actually says what the claim implies; a data schema cannot do that.

Independence remains partly self-attested. Source ownership can be misdeclared, and source tiers do
not prove reliability. Archive hashes detect changed local bytes but cannot authenticate a publisher.
Source truth, statement semantics and selective omission remain required human review work.

## 11. Executive UX Review

The initial five minutes begin with setup commands and a file tour. A busy reader instead needs the
proposed choice, cap, downside, best alternative and weakest evidence. The new EXECUTIVE_START_HERE
route leads to the compact worked brief, then summary and weakest dependency. The next ten minutes
trace a claim to supporting/counterevidence and inspect the action gate.

The completed synthetic brief is useful and bounded, but Markdown does not guarantee a printed
one-page layout. Wide evidence tables remain drilldown material. Software setup belongs in the full
repository. No external executive usability test or rendered report study was conducted.

## 12. Analyst UX Review

The required sequence is source → evidence/claim/map → domain objects → factor bases → calculation
→ validation/source challenge → narratives → sign-off → package. The initializer removes a risky
manual clearing process without changing the foundation. It does not generate real research.

Factor bases increase entry work but explain ratings that previously had no provenance. Exact headers,
source snapshots and many CSV joins still create friction. Release tests are intentionally sample-based;
a live engagement must not interpret them as fact checks. Validation and project review are distinct.
Repeated source revisions need manual supersession and archival discipline until a lifecycle model ships.

## 13. Technical Review

The tools are small, inspectable and network-free, but the baseline assumes well-formed policy metadata
and largely tests the demonstration. Boundary validation now precedes references/calculation. Source
errors have controlled CLI status. Shipped schemas execute a closed supported keyword subset and reject
unsupported extensions instead of silently ignoring them. This is not a full Draft 2020-12 engine.

Refresh now stages changed files, records recovery state and rolls back raised write failures.
Validation refuses an interrupted transaction. This is a single-writer recovery protocol, not a
database transaction or concurrency merge service. In-memory CSV processing and repeated joins limit
scale. Python 3.12.14/Windows is the executed environment; broader support needs a separate matrix.

## 14. Security & Privacy Review

The strongest reproduced exposure is unintended file distribution, not a network attack: tools do not
fetch URLs. Unlisted notes previously leaked by directory copy. The allowlist closes that route and
source archives default to private in live mode. Path traversal is refused, credential URLs cannot
be retained and viewing CSVs neutralize formula text. Hashes and metadata are verified for exact coverage.

SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md now covers pseudonymous customer records, restricted raw stores, sensitive
commercial data, rights/consent, OS access control/encryption, temporary files, logs, export review and
incident response. The software does not enforce encryption or authenticate signers. A trusted local
operator can edit code, fabricate an attestation or omit material evidence. Those limits must remain
explicit. No source quote or private research was sent externally during this audit.

Current guides explain model migration, software vs reading packages, factor bases, live sign-off and

## 18. False Precision Risks

Two decimals are arithmetic precision, not measurement precision. Different factor weights, overlapping
inputs and subjective 0–5 anchors can move an index more than the displayed hundredths. Claim confidence
is not a probability. QA coverage is not assurance. Ordinal likelihood is not an actuarial frequency.
Executive views use whole-point score displays with category and gates; exact stored numbers remain
auditable. A rank should be described as fragile when ±1 input changes its order or decision category.
No confidence interval is invented from these ordinal ratings.

## 19. Trust Risks

Residual risks are dishonest attestations, omitted critical assumptions, incorrect source ownership,
semantic misquotation, poor sampling and stale interpretations retained without supersession. Neither
a URL nor a hash verifies a publisher. A named sign-off is local attestation, not cryptographic authority.
Reserved-host/known-marker checks catch specified relabeling paths but cannot identify every fabricated
source. Disclosure, independent research challenge and organizational authority remain essential.
