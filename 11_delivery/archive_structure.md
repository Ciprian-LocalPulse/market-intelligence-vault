# Archive structure

Store immutable snapshots under engagement/version/as-of-date/ with the report, canonical CSVs,
model CSVs, config, source archives allowed by rights, approval record, QA log and checksums.

Use restricted access for confidential customer evidence. Maintain an index of prior decisions,
superseded claims and changed recommendations. Retain corrections and retractions. Verify hashes
after transfer. Record retention/deletion policy and source usage restrictions before archiving.
The package builder creates a new folder only and never deletes a prior snapshot.
