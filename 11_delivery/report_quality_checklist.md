# Report quality checklist

- [ ] Requested decision and allocation are clear in the first page.
- [ ] Material claims have source locators and reviewed confidence.
- [ ] Market scope, price periods and customer sampling limits are comparable.
- [ ] Contradictions and missing evidence appear beside their conclusions.
- [ ] Formula recomputation and score audit pass at the delivery date.
- [ ] Recommendation status matches the approval record.
- [ ] Stage gates, metrics, owners and stop rules are usable.
- [ ] License, limitations and source rights are included.
- [ ] Completed report contains no unresolved placeholders.
- [ ] All ten QA categories and hard gates are signed.
