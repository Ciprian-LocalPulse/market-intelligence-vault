# Final report structure

1. Decision brief: requested commitment, why now and alternatives.
2. Executive summary: five findings, three opportunities/risks, confidence and next actions.
3. Market: scope, spend estimates, growth, maturity, drivers and barriers.
4. Competition: task-based profiles, price comparability and response risk.
5. Customers: jobs, observed behavior, sampling limits and commercial validation.
6. Opportunities: raw/adjusted scores, gap evidence, sensitivity and gates.
7. Risks: horizon, exposure, mitigation, unknowns and acceptance authority.
8. Strategy: proposed/approved option, upside/downside, resource cap and action plan.
9. Appendices: source library, evidence map, methods, score versions and research debt.

Every numerical headline includes units, period, label and traceable inputs. Assemble narrative
sections from reviewed templates; the generated summary cannot replace completed analysis.
