# Executive readout outline

| Minutes | Topic | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Decision and capital/time exposure | Agree on requested authority |
| 3–8 | Five findings and weakest dependencies | Understand what is known and uncertain |
| 8–13 | Opportunities, threats and alternatives | Compare feasible choices |
| 13–17 | Recommended test and stop rules | Agree on measures and resource cap |
| 17–20 | Approval and next 30 days | Assign authority, owners and review date |

Keep the evidence map available for challenge questions. Record dissent and the evidence that
would change the decision. Do not imply a proposal is approved because the readout occurred.
