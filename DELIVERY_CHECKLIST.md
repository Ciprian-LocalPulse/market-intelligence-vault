# Delivery checklist

- [ ] Scope, geography, period and decision horizon are explicit.
- [ ] Every material recommendation links to claims, evidence and source locators.
- [ ] FACT / INFERENCE / ESTIMATE / HYPOTHESIS / RECOMMENDATION labels are preserved.
- [ ] Synthetic content is excluded from live delivery and visible in foundation delivery.
- [ ] Source rights, archive references and independence reviewed.
- [ ] Conflicts, unknowns, dependencies and research debt remain visible.
- [ ] Scores recomputed at the fixed as-of date; audit and tests pass.
- [ ] Risk owners, acceptance authority and commitment gates documented.
- [ ] Executive templates completed; placeholders removed for live reports.
- [ ] Actions have owners, metrics, dependencies, status and stop rules.
- [ ] QA score and hard-gate review signed.
- [ ] Delivery manifest and checksums verified; archive stored with access controls.
