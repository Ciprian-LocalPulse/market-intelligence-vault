# Decision framework

## Feasibility before ranking

List the feasible options, horizon and resource envelope. Exclude options that breach a verified
constraint or carry an unacceptable unmitigated consequence. Compare the remaining options using
the decision matrix. A score can prioritize review; only recorded authority can approve a choice.

| Option | Appropriate condition | Required evidence / gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ENTER | A scoped market supports a feasible offer and channel | Live paid-demand evidence, economics, capacity, compliance and risk acceptance |
| TEST | A material unknown can be resolved cheaply and reversibly | Discriminating test, owner, capped budget, metric, stop rule and staged authority |
| EXPAND | Existing delivery produces repeat paid value | Cohort outcomes, retained contribution, capacity and quality under scale |
| PARTNER | Another organization can close a verified access/capability gap | Terms, incentive alignment, dependency risk, diligence and fallback |
| ACQUIRE | Control of assets/capabilities is justified over alternatives | Independent diligence, verified financials, integration case and transaction authority |
| DIFFERENTIATE | A measured customer outcome can improve on alternatives | Matched task proof, willingness to pay, cost to maintain and response risk |
| REPOSITION | Current value promise is weak or no longer relevant | Segment/job evidence, message behavior, product-fit test and transition cost |
| WAIT | Timing or a blocking prerequisite prevents a useful commitment | Named unblock condition, holding cost, opportunity cost and review date |
| MONITOR | Evidence is insufficient and immediate testing has low decision value | Observable promotion trigger, source, owner and review cadence |
| AVOID | Downside or economics remains unacceptable within the scope | Explicit failed constraint, best alternative and condition for reconsideration |

Confidence below 60 requires research; 60–<75 supports validation only; >=75 permits a commitment
review after the remaining gates pass. A Critical risk cannot be compensated for by opportunity
appeal. New irreversible commitments require current live evidence and named acceptance authority.

## Decision record

Record decision ID, option, scope, rationale, supporting claims, evidence, weakest confidence,
expected upside, major downside, alternative, capital/time cap, next validation step, owner and
proposal/approval status. Show what would reverse the recommendation. An estimate belongs with
its formula and assumptions. A proposal remains a proposal in every output until approved.

## Gate meeting

Review the weakest dependency first. Check contradictions, research debt and changed source
conditions. Evaluate the downside case, then compare upside. Record dissent, unresolved issues,
authority and the next review date. If a prerequisite fails, stop, reduce scope or select WAIT.
Retain the original proposal and record why the final decision changed.
